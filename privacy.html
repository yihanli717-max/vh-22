Last updated: 2026-05-10

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BizTrack collects, uses, discloses and safeguards your information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to operate and improve the website, respond to inquiries, provide features, analyze usage patterns, and comply with legal obligations.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use cookies for essential site functionality, to remember preferences (such as language), and to collect anonymous analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

Third-party Services

We may use third-party services (e.g., analytics providers). These providers have their own privacy practices — please refer to their policies for details.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or to comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
